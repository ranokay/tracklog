# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - Unreleased

- Scrobbles what plays on this Mac to Last.fm, ListenBrainz, and Libre.fm or another GNU FM server. Capture is off until you turn it on, and it runs while Droppy is open.
- Keeps every listen in a local history that works with no service connected, and queues deliveries while you are offline.
- Shelf widget with the current track, love, edit, cancel and block. History takeover with date jump, track history, local edit and delete, and the pending queue.
- Charts of top artists, albums and tracks from this Mac's history or the current service account, with saved results and manual refresh.
- Catalogue search with a search-and-play URL template, loved tracks, and artist, album and track pages with your own image overrides.
- Simple metadata edits, blocks, per-player allow list, pause, and a settings export that contains no credentials.
