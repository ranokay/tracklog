the jump anchor (`Blue Monday, 21:18`) in the list. The older-day rows stayed

window reached the newest stored listen the jump bar disappeared and the
regular feed remained; the Activity page recorded the earlier
`present "history"` request. Repeated newer loads produced no duplicate rows.
