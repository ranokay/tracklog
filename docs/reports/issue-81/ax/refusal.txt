# AX readout — Jump to date refused by the harness

Fixture: `TRACKLOG_DEMO_STATE=playing` (normal History), Expanded surface page.

- Pressing `AXButton (Jump to date)` (125 × 31) calls
  `host.workspace.openSettingsPage("history", title: "History")`.
- The harness workspace service refuses that call. The takeover stays up and
  shows:
  - `AXStaticText = "Settings could not be opened here. Open Tracklog's
    History settings to choose a date."`.
  - `AXButton (Dismiss)`.
- The pending jump is retained for the next open. The refusal is not a silent
  no-op and the feed remains usable.
- In the real host, `openSettingsPage` is expected to succeed and dismiss the
  takeover; that host result was not observed and is recorded as not passed.
