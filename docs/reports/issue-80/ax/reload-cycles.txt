# Repeated harness Reload on a loading chart

Fixture: `TRACKLOG_DEMO_STATE=playing TRACKLOG_HARNESS_CHARTS=loading TRACKLOG_HARNESS_SECTION=charts`.
The harness playback strip showed `Heart of Glass — Blondie`.

1. Opened `Expanded surface`, then pressed `AXButton "Reload"` three times.
2. Immediately after the third reload:
   - `AXHeading "Charts"` in all three surface copies
   - `AXButton = "First load" (Refresh charts)`
   - `AXBusyIndicator (Loading charts)`
   - `AXStaticText "Loading top artists…"`
   - `AXStaticText "Reading Last.fm · listener. Your listening keeps recording."`
   - The playing strip remained visible.
   - No rows were shown before the delayed response.
3. After waiting past the fixture's 30-second delay:
   - `AXUnknown (Rank 1, Blondie, 42 plays)` appeared in all three copies.
   - `AXStaticText "Saved just now"` and the Refresh button value updated.
   - No mixed rows or stale source appeared.
4. `Activity` page: the visible host-call list contained HUD present/dismiss
   calls; a search for `error` and `Tracklog activated` returned no matching
   AX text. The harness chrome showed `Active`.
5. Interactive harness mode has no `report.json`. The corresponding
   fixture-backed gate run `charts-loading` reports `problems: []`,
   `activation.active=true`, `activation.error=null` in
   `docs/reports/issue-80/reports/charts-loading.json`.

No activation error, late early publish or visible report problem was observed
in the interactive run.
