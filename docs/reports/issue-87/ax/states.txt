# AX readout — Loved loading, empty, unsupported, failed and no connection

Each state was a fresh harness launch with
`TRACKLOG_DEMO_STATE=playing TRACKLOG_HARNESS_SECTION=loved` and the named
`TRACKLOG_HARNESS_LOVED` fixture. Excerpts are the first (notch) takeover.

## `loading`

- `AXBusyIndicator` `Loading loved tracks`.
- `AXStaticText` `Loading loved tracks…`.
- The header keeps `Loved`, the source label `Last.fm · listener`, the
  connection popup and `Refresh loved tracks`.

## `empty`

- `AXStaticText` `No loved tracks on Last.fm`.
- `AXStaticText` `Tracks you love there appear here.`
- No rows, no `Unlove` buttons. This is a successful empty read, not an error.

## `unsupported` (custom ListenBrainz-compatible root)

- `AXPopUpButton` value `ListenBrainz · listener`.
- `AXStaticText` `Loved tracks unavailable`.
- `AXStaticText` `This ListenBrainz-compatible server does not provide loved tracks.`
- `AXButton` `Open Services`.

## `failed`

- `AXStaticText` `Loved tracks could not be loaded`.
- `AXStaticText` `Last.fm could not be reached. Check your connection and try again.`
- `AXButton` `Try again`.
- The source label still names the account the failed read belonged to.

## `no-connection`

- `AXStaticText` `Current connection, none` (the header's switcher).
- `AXStaticText` `Loved tracks unavailable`.
- `AXStaticText` `Loved tracks come from the current connection. Choose
  Last.fm, Libre.fm or ListenBrainz in the switcher above, or connect one on
  Services.`
- `AXButton` `Open Services`.
- No rows: the state never pretends the account has no loved tracks.

The remaining required states — needs sign-in, rejected credential, paused
with its reason bar, the per-row action failure and its Dismiss, refresh
failure over rows, the rate-limit wait and metadata unavailable — are in
`required-states.txt` with their harness fixtures.

## Unreachable in this build

- A Pleroma connection has no `ServiceKind` yet (#97 owns additional service
  connections), so its unavailable state cannot be created through the UI.
  The read model still refuses any kind without a reader with the same
  unavailable treatment; the custom-root fixture above is the reachable
  unsupported path.
