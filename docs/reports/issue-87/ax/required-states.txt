# AX readout — Required Loved states

Each state was a fresh harness launch with
`TRACKLOG_DEMO_STATE=playing TRACKLOG_HARNESS_SECTION=loved` and the named
`TRACKLOG_HARNESS_LOVED` fixture. Excerpts are the first (notch) takeover; the
harness repeats it at the Dynamic Island and camera geometries.

## `needs-sign-in`

- `AXPopUpButton` value `Last.fm · listener`.
- `AXStaticText` `Last.fm needs sign-in. Sign in on Services to browse its loved tracks.`
- `AXButton` `Try again`.
- `AXButton` `Open Services`.
- No rows.

## `credential-rejected`

- `AXStaticText` `Last.fm rejected the saved credential. Sign in on Services.`
- `AXButton` `Try again`, `AXButton` `Open Services`.
- No rows. The stored connection still reports signed in on Services; only the
  next feedback/delivery may transition it.

## `paused`

- `AXStaticText` `Last.fm is paused. Turn it on in Services to unlove tracks.`
- `AXButton` `Open Services`.
- Each row: `AXUnknown` `Example Track One, Example Artist, Example Album`
  with the help text `Last.fm is paused. Turn it on in Services to unlove
  tracks.`; no `Unlove` button is present.

## `action-failure`

- `AXStaticText` `Could not unlove “Example Track One” on Last.fm. The service did not confirm the change.`
- `AXButton` `Dismiss`.
- The row `Example Track One` and its `Unlove Example Track One` button remain.
- Pressed `Dismiss`: the static text and button disappear; the row and its
  button remain. Screenshot: `loved-action-dismissed.png`.

## `refresh-failure`

- `AXStaticText` `Could not refresh loved tracks. Showing the last loaded list. Last.fm could not be reached. Check your connection and try again.`
- `AXButton` `Try again`.
- Rows `Example Track One` … remain, with their `Unlove` buttons.

## `rate-limited`

- `AXStaticText` `Last.fm is rate limiting requests. Try again after 16:42.`
- `AXButton` `Refresh loved tracks` is present but disabled.
- No `Try again` button and no rows. The wait is shown instead of an ignored
  immediate retry.

## `metadata-unavailable`

- `AXPopUpButton` value `ListenBrainz · listener`.
- `AXStaticText` `Loved tracks need metadata`.
- `AXStaticText` `ListenBrainz has loved recordings for this account, but none of the first 2 could be matched to track metadata.`
- No rows. The state never says the account has no loved tracks.
