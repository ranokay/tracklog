# AX readout — Search palette, keyboard walk

Fixture: `TRACKLOG_DEMO_STATE=playing TRACKLOG_HARNESS_PALETTE=normal`.
The panel was opened and the query `blo` settled to ten artists, ten albums,
ten tracks and eight loved rows. The field stayed focused throughout.

1. `down × 5`: the selection moved from Blondie to Blondie cover band; no
   accessibility focus moved out of the field.
2. `down`, then `return` on `Show more artists`: the group expanded from five
   to ten rows and the selection moved to the first newly revealed row (Bloc).
   The Show more row disappeared from the AX tree.
3. `up × 6`, then `return` on `Artists`: the header changed from
   `Expanded. 10 results` to `Collapsed`; its ten rows left the AX tree.
4. `escape` with `blo` in the field: the field value became empty, the panel
   stayed open, and `Type an artist, album or track name to search the Last.fm
   catalogue.` appeared.
5. `escape` again: the panel was removed from the window list.
6. A foreground click on the harness window closed the panel, confirming the
   outside-click close path.

The marked-input-method guard is covered by
`PaletteKeyRouterTests.aMarkedInputMethodTextKeepsEveryPaletteKeyInTheFieldEditor`
rather than a live IME run.
